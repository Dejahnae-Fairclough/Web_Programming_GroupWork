About Meadowbrook

Ita Splendeat Lvx Vestra — "Let Your Light So Shine"

Our History

A Legacy of Excellence

Established in 1958, Meadowbrook High School has built a strong reputation for academic achievement and personal development in Kingston, Jamaica.

Our motto, Ita Splendeat Lvx Vestra (Let Your Light So Shine), from Matthew 5:16, reflects the values that guide our school. We encourage every student to develop their talents and reach their potential.

We offer opportunities in science, humanities, business, technology and the arts while promoting leadership, creativity, good character and community involvement.

Today, Meadowbrook continues to provide a supportive environment where students from different backgrounds can gain the knowledge and skills needed to succeed.
