FORMER PRINCIPAL MICHAEL PEART

Meadowbrook High School creates a space where students are able to strive for excellence in the arts, academics, service and sports. Meadowbrook has become a family for children from diverse backgrounds and communities, guided by the ethos “Ita Splendeat Lvx Vestra” — “Let Your Light So Shine”, taken from Matthew 5:16.

THE MEADOWBROOK WAY

Four commitments. One community.

These are not slogans on a wall. They are the daily disciplines that shape every classroom, corridor, court and chapel service on our campus.

01

Academic
excellence

CSEC and CAPE preparation across the sciences, humanities, business, technology and the arts, taught by subject specialists in thirteen departments.

02

Character &
leadership

We form young people of conscience and courage through prefectship, mentoring, faith and a strong code of conduct.

03

Vibrant student life

Seven sports, fifteen clubs and groups, four houses, the performing arts, debate and robotics.

04

Community &
service

Rooted in our community and proud of our nation — service, outreach and partnerships shape every Meadowbrook student.
