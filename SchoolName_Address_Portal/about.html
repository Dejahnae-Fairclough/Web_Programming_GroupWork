About Meadowbrook

Ita Splendeat Lvx Vestra — "Let Your Light So Shine"

Our History

A Legacy of Excellence

Established in 1958, Meadowbrook High School has spent decades building a strong reputation for academic achievement and personal development in Kingston, Jamaica. What started as a humble institution has grown into a school that is committed to helping students discover their potential and succeed in all areas of life.

Our motto, Ita Splendeat Lvx Vestra (Let Your Light So Shine), taken from Matthew 5:16, reflects the values that continue to guide our school community. We believe that every student has something special to offer, and we strive to create an environment where they can develop their talents, build confidence and reach their full potential.

Over the years, Meadowbrook has continued to grow, offering students opportunities to explore a wide range of subjects, including science, humanities, business, technology and the arts. We believe education goes beyond the classroom, which is why we also encourage leadership, good character, creativity and active involvement in the community.

Today, Meadowbrook High School remains a welcoming and supportive community for students from different backgrounds. As we continue to build on our history, our goal remains the same: to provide students with the knowledge, values and skills they need to make a difference in the world.
